How It Works:

Letter Grade Number Grade Unweighted Points Weighted Points
A+/A/A- 90-100+ 4.0 5.0
B+/B/B- 80-89 3.0 4.0
C+/C/C- 70-79 2.0 3.0
D+/D/D- 60-69 1.0 2.0
F 0-59 0.0 1.0
  1. The letter/number grade is converted to points on the 4.0 or 5.0 scale.
  2. The points for each class are added up.
  3. The sum of points is divided by the number of classes to determine the GPA (Grade Point Average).
